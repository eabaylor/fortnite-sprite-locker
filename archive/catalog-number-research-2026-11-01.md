# Missing entry and number research

Owner request: search online for numbered Sprite images, add numbers, and find the missing entry in the 33-entry checklist.

Missing entry: Cursed Kelper. Its identity and artwork are represented in multiple online catalogs, including https://fortgacha.com/events/fn-sprite-checklist-c7-treasure-hunters , https://fnsprites.info/catalog-season-5.html , and https://fnspritetracker.net/ . Its Fortnite.GG artwork reference is T_Icon_BR_Creature_Sprite_UnbotheredFish_Cursed_L.webp. Added to the owner's requested in-game collection list; availability remains potentially time-gated.

Image searches and public catalog metadata did not establish a reliable complete in-game numbering table. The public character icons inspected contain no printed numbers. Websites also disagree on counts (22, 31, 33, or 35) and use their own ordering; neither their database IDs nor array positions are treated as Fortnite collection numbers.

Only the ten PlayStation numbers supplied by the owner are displayed. Unverified Base, Fortune, Sweet Dreams, Transponder Snail and Kelper numbers remain blank. No numbers are inferred by subtracting one/two from a Cursed number or extrapolating gaps. Season progress and archived share dictionaries remain unchanged.
